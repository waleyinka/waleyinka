### 🧭 About Me

I didn't start in data. I started in product design, then spent years trying to fix broken supply chains from the outside before deciding to fix them from the data layer instead.

- 🌾 My first venture was in **farming/agriculture** — it didn't work, but it taught me where the real bottlenecks in a supply chain live
- 🚚 My second was a **farm-to-consumer food delivery startup**, connecting fresh produce to buyers and cutting post-harvest loss for farmers — shut down when COVID broke the access-to-produce chain it depended on
- 💬 I moved into **fintech**, supporting customers at **Wise**, sitting close to the data that explains why things go wrong for users
- 🔁 Now I'm a **Data & Analytics Engineer / DBA in training**, ~6 years into a career spanning FinTech and EdTech, rebuilding my design instincts as data-modeling instincts

I build almost everything in public — daily SQL/Pandas drills, pipeline write-ups, and lab notes that turn into LinkedIn and Medium posts.
